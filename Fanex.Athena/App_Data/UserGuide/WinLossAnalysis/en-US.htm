General Info

  • This report is reported gross revenue, have not yet deducted cost, fee, ect...
  • Total Bonus/Deposit/Withdrawal statistics will be affected by changing Date, Brand, Username, Currency and Timezone.
  • Player Closing Balance can be displayed in GMT +7 and GMT +8 only, and support data from 03/05/2021 GMT+00.
  • There are some limitations to display Commission for 9W Sports.
    • If the report is filtered by Settled Date, can display commission on the related columns (Commission, GGR).
    • If filtered by Created Date, cannot display commission (Commission = 0.00).
    • For Bet List, cannot display commission info (Commission = 0.00).
